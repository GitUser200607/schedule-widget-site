Calendar and tasks widget for Android

What this app is

ScheduleWidget is a home screen widget for Android. It shows, in one scrolling list, the calendar events of the current device and the tasks from the user's Google Tasks account, so the day can be read without opening any other app.

What it shows

  • today, tomorrow and the following days — events with their time and calendar colour;
  • Google Tasks with their due dates, including overdue ones, and an expandable list of the oldest overdue tasks;
  • birthdays taken from the device contacts;
  • the next alarm set on the device, when there is one.

Who it is for

A personal app for one user — its author. It is installed manually (sideloaded) and is not distributed through app stores.

How it uses Google data

The app requests two read-only Google scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks.readonly — to list the user's unfinished Google Tasks with their due dates;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly — to read the colours of the user's calendars.

Access is granted through the Google authorization panel. The app receives a short-lived access token from Google Play services and calls only tasks.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com. No token or Google data is sent to any server of the author; the results are cached locally on the device so the widget keeps working offline. Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Calendar, contacts and tasks are read on the device and stay on the device.
